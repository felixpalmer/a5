Fields of The World

by Taylor Geospatial

Over 3 billion agricultural field boundaries, mapped from Sentinel-2 imagery for every year from 2017 to 2025. A5 cells are used to provide aggregated overviews at low zooms. Data served using PMTiles, generated with TylerToo.

  • GeoParquet
  • TylerToo
  • PMTiles

A5 Pyramids

by Belian Earth

AlphaEarth Foundations satellite embeddings for Sabah, Borneo, resampled to A5 cells and colored by the principal components of whatever is on screen. A5 Cell pyramids stream Parquet tiles and are colored on the GPU using a custom deck.gl layer, with no tile server needed.

  • AlphaEarth
  • Parquet
  • a5px
Open ↗

FIRMS Explorer

by Portolan

NASA FIRMS active fire detections since November 2000, with real-time spatial and temporal filtering and aggregation to A5 cells visualized using deck.gl A5Layer. Built and served using Portolan as a STAC catalog of GeoParquet files with no server.

  • GeoParquet
  • STAC
  • Portolan
  • deck.gl
